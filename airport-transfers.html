Transfer FAQ

Before you land

Can I book a late-night or early-morning pickup?

Yes. Send your arrival time and route early so the driver, vehicle type, and contact details can be confirmed before you travel.

What should I send for airport pickup?

Send arrival date, flight number, passenger count, luggage size, drop-off location, and the WhatsApp number we should use for updates.

Can you combine airport transfer with hotel booking?

Yes. Augur can coordinate your hotel, airport transfer, and onward tour or vehicle hire together for a smoother arrival plan.

Entebbe to Kampala

A clear plan from arrivals to your destination.

For an Entebbe airport transfer to Kampala, share your flight arrival time, hotel or drop-off address, passenger count and luggage. For a return transfer, include your collection time and flight departure details.

Choose the vehicle

Ask which vehicle fits your passengers and luggage. Mention child-seat needs or accessibility requirements when requesting availability.

Know the full cost

Ask the team to state the vehicle, one-way or return fare, currency, fuel, airport parking and any waiting or extra-stop charges.

Confirm your pickup

Agree the meeting point, driver contact and arrangements for a delayed flight before travelling. Journey time varies with traffic and your destination.
