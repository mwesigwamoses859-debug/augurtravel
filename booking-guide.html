Understand what happens between your first enquiry and your confirmed journey.

Request a quotation ↗How booking works

1. Share your plans

Send your preferred dates, destinations, group size and travel style. For transfers, include pickup, drop-off and flight information where relevant.

2. Review the proposed arrangements

The team checks availability and prepares a quotation. Ask about included activities, accommodation, meals, transport, permits and any excluded costs.

3. Agree the details in writing

Before paying, confirm the itinerary, total cost and currency, payment schedule, cancellation conditions and the company payment instructions directly with Augur. These details depend on the proposed trip and supplier arrangements.

4. Receive your confirmation

A website enquiry or a WhatsApp message is a request. The team confirms your arrangements in writing once the relevant requirements are met. Confirm pickup contacts, meeting points and any necessary travel documents before departure.

Need to change your plans?

Contact the team with your booking reference and proposed changes. Ask which supplier conditions or costs apply before accepting a change.
