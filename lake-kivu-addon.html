Questions

Lake Kivu Add-on FAQ

When does Lake Kivu fit best?

It works well after Kigali city tours, gorilla trekking, Nyungwe forest visits, or Uganda-Rwanda cross-border routes.

Can you arrange hotels and transport?

Yes. Share your route, travel month, number of guests, and comfort level so Augur can plan transport and accommodation.

Is it only a boat trip?

No. It can be a short boat experience, a relaxed lakeside stay, or a scenic extension inside a longer Rwanda itinerary.
