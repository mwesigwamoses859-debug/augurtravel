3 day Murchison Falls safari from Kampala

Your starting budget

3-day Murchison Falls safari

Guide from US$750 per person

Based on 2 adults sharing one twin or double room, travelling together on a private road safari from Kampala or Entebbe. Accommodation: budget lodge / guesthouse.

Included in this guide

  • Return road transport from one agreed Kampala or Entebbe pickup point, with fuel and an English-speaking driver-guide.
  • Two nights in the stated accommodation category, sharing a twin or double room. The exact property and room are named in your quotation.
  • Meals from lunch on day 1 through lunch on day 3, plus drinking water during the road safari.
  • The activities and admission charges listed for this package.
  • Game drive, Nile cruise and falls viewpoint; Park entry and Ziwa rhino tracking.

Quoted separately

International and domestic flights, visas, travel insurance, tips, personal spending, drinks other than the stated water, single-room supplements and activities not listed as included.

Ziwa rhino tracking is included in this planning guide. Budongo chimpanzee tracking, extra nights and private boat upgrades are quoted separately.

Planning estimate researched 5 November 2026, using adult foreign nonresident admission rates. Guide for travel through December 2026; other dates and traveller categories are recosted. No lodge, vehicle or permit is reserved by this page. Your written quotation confirms availability, named accommodation, total price, payment schedule and cancellation conditions before you pay.

Sample Flow

Simple 3-day itinerary idea

Day 1

Depart Kampala or Entebbe, include Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary as shown in the price guide, continue to Murchison Falls, and settle in near the park.

Day 2

Morning game drive for giraffes, elephants, hartebeests, buffaloes, antelopes, and patas monkeys, then take a Nile boat cruise.

Day 3

Visit the falls viewpoint, enjoy final photography stops where time allows, then return to Kampala or Entebbe.

Can Add

Bird watching, sport fishing by arrangement, Budongo chimpanzee plans, or an extra night for a slower family pace.

Questions

Murchison Falls Safari FAQ

Can you pick us from Entebbe Airport?

Yes. Share your arrival time, group size, and luggage details so Augur can plan airport pickup and the right vehicle.

Can Ziwa be added on the same route?

Yes. Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary works well as a stopover between Kampala or Entebbe and Murchison Falls.

How much is the safari?

The starting guide is US$750 per person for 2 adults sharing on a budget-lodge private road safari. Dates, room choice and group size affect your final written quotation.
