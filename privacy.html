Enquiry privacy

Your travel enquiry

The guided booking enquiry asks for the information needed to discuss your trip: travel preferences, dates, group size, name and contact details. Optional information is marked in the form.

Sending your enquiry

The direct enquiry form is provided through Google Forms. When you press Submit, your name, contact details, service choice and travel requirements are stored in a private response register managed for Augur, and an alert is sent to the form owner. The form does not require a Google account and does not share the response register with other travellers. Google processes the form under its own privacy terms.

The optional detailed request builder prepares a draft in the open page. If you choose WhatsApp or email, review and send the message in that app. A submitted enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Website measurements

We count page views, enquiry-form views, quotation-button clicks, WhatsApp clicks and telephone clicks by service, general referral source and day. These aggregate measurements do not contain your name, phone number, email address, travel details or the full address of the page you visited. The measurement tool does not use tracking cookies. Received enquiries, quotations and confirmed bookings are recorded separately by the team.

WhatsApp, email and other links

WhatsApp, your email provider, Tripadvisor, SafariBookings and Google Maps operate under their own privacy terms. A map may load when you open the office location panel. Other trip-planning tools may save a brief on your device when you explicitly choose to save it.

Keep your request relevant

Please do not put card details, passport scans or passwords in the enquiry form. Discuss any documents needed for a confirmed trip directly with the team.
