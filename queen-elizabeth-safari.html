Sample Flow

Simple 3-day itinerary idea

Day 1

Drive from Kampala or Entebbe through western Uganda, arrive near Queen Elizabeth National Park, and settle in.

Day 2

Take a Kasenyi game drive, watch for elephant herds and antelopes, then enjoy the Kazinga Channel boat cruise.

Day 3

Add crater lake viewpoints, salt pan scenery, or a slower return drive depending on the group's schedule.

Can Add

Ishasha tree-climbing lions, Kyambura Gorge, Kibale chimpanzees, or a Bwindi gorilla extension.

Questions

Queen Elizabeth Safari FAQ

Is the Kazinga Channel boat cruise included?

It can be included in the quote. Share your travel dates so Augur can check timing and plan the route properly.

Can Ishasha be added?

Yes. Ishasha is best added when the group has enough time or is continuing toward Bwindi or south-western Uganda.

Do you provide driver-guides?

Yes. Augur can arrange driver-guided transport suited to the road conditions, passenger count, and luggage.
