Permits & Availability

Secure limited activities before locking the rest of the route

Permit rules, prices, and availability can change. Augur confirms current information with the relevant authority before payment.

Most time-sensitive

Gorilla trekking

Plan early for Bwindi, Mgahinga, or Rwanda's Volcanoes region—especially in popular travel months. A permit is tied to a specific date and trekking area.

Recommended: enquire before booking flights.
Popular forest activity

Chimpanzee tracking

Kibale and other forest experiences have scheduled sessions and limited spaces. Habituation experiences generally need more time than standard tracking.

Recommended: confirm session and route together.
Availability check

Boat trips & special activities

Kazinga Channel cruises, Nile boats, sport fishing, and specialist guides should be checked against park timing and the group's road schedule.

Recommended: avoid assuming same-day space.

When To Travel

Every season has a trade-off

Dec-Feb

Drier window

Popular for wildlife routes and trekking. Dust and demand can be higher.

Mar-May

Longer rains

Greener scenery and fewer visitors, with muddier trails and slower roads possible.

Jun-Sep

Peak safari season

Often easier road conditions and strong demand. Early planning is valuable.

Oct-Nov

Shorter rains

Lush landscapes and variable showers. Flexible pacing helps.

Packing

Pack for changing terrain, not just the forecast

Trekking

  • Broken-in walking boots
  • Long trousers and sleeves
  • Light rain jacket
  • Daypack and water bottle
  • Gardening-style gloves if preferred

Game drives

  • Neutral lightweight layers
  • Warm early-morning layer
  • Sun protection
  • Binoculars
  • Camera batteries and dust protection

Documents

  • Passport and entry documents
  • Travel insurance details
  • Required health certificates
  • Permit confirmation
  • Emergency contacts and copies

Travel Essentials

Important details to verify before departure

Entry and health requirements change. Always confirm with official government and health sources for your nationality and itinerary.

Visas and border crossings

Check current Uganda and Rwanda entry rules, passport validity, visa type, and East Africa itinerary requirements with official immigration sources. Allow extra time for cross-border road days.

Health and vaccinations

Speak with a qualified travel-health professional about yellow fever documentation, malaria prevention, routine vaccines, personal medication, and your specific route.

Money and payments

Carry a practical mix of payment methods. Ask which trip costs are included, which are payable locally, and whether card fees or cash limits apply.

Families and children

Some activities have minimum ages or demanding terrain. Share children's ages early so the itinerary can use suitable rooms, drive lengths, and activities.

Accessibility and mobility

Tell Augur about mobility, medical, sensory, or dietary needs before confirming. The team can discuss vehicle access, lodge layout, terrain, and realistic alternatives.

Travel insurance

Choose coverage appropriate for safari activities, medical treatment, evacuation, cancellation, baggage, and any trekking or adventure components.
