Questions

Ziwa Rhino Tracking FAQ

Can this be done in one day?

Yes. It is a practical day trip from Kampala, and it can also work as a stopover toward Murchison Falls.

Is it suitable for school groups?

Yes. The conservation learning angle makes Ziwa useful for school trips, youth groups, and educational travel.

What should we send for a quote?

Send pickup point, date, number of guests, school or private group details, and whether the trip continues to Murchison.
